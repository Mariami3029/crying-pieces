Crying Walls

Add a photo of each poster or sticker. Tap the photo where the tears should start (eyes, a crack, the top edge), then write what it cries. If you don't tap anywhere, it cries from the top edge.

Photograph posters straight on, in even light. Busy, detailed images track best. Building the targets takes a little while; doing it on a laptop is faster.
